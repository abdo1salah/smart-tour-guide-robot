LANGUAGE: en
MUSEUM: GEM
EXHIBIT: Canopic Chest
PERIOD: New Kingdom, Dynasty 18
TYPE: details
TAGS: gold foil, ritual shrine, royal imagery, canopic equipment

TEXT:
The chest features a sloping lid once secured with linen cords and rests on a wooden sledge covered in gold foil. Inside are four cylindrical compartments, each holding a solid gold miniature coffin. The cavities are sealed with stoppers carved as royal heads with painted facial details.

MUSEUM: GEM
EXHIBIT: Canopic Chest
PERIOD: New Kingdom, Dynasty 18
TYPE: significance
TAGS: embalming, afterlife, royal regeneration, funerary belief

TEXT:
Canopic chests were essential to royal burials, housing the embalmed organs believed necessary for the king’s spiritual regeneration. Tutankhamun’s chest reflects the renewed emphasis on traditional funerary beliefs following the Amarna period, highlighting divine protection and ritual completeness.
