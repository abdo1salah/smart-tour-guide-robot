TITLE: Royal Diadem of Tutankhamun – Overview
LANGUAGE: en
MUSEUM: GEM
EXHIBIT: Royal Diadem
PERIOD: New Kingdom, Dynasty 18 (Tutankhamun)
TYPE: overview
TAGS: Tutankhamun, royal regalia, diadem, protective magic

TEXT:
The Royal Diadem is a gold headband found encircling Tutankhamun’s head beneath the funerary mask. It secured the king’s wig and provided divine protection.



ID: gem_tut_royal_diadem_details_en_002
TITLE: Royal Diadem of Tutankhamun – Design
LANGUAGE: en
MUSEUM: GEM
EXHIBIT: Royal Diadem
PERIOD: New Kingdom, Dynasty 18 (Tutankhamun)
TYPE: details
TAGS: gold, carnelian, uraeus, vulture goddess

TEXT:
The fillet is decorated with carnelian roundels and blue glass squares. At the front are removable figures of the vulture goddess Nekhbet and the cobra goddess Wadjet.



ID: gem_tut_royal_diadem_function_en_003
TITLE: Royal Diadem of Tutankhamun – Symbolism
LANGUAGE: en
MUSEUM: GEM
EXHIBIT: Royal Diadem
PERIOD: New Kingdom, Dynasty 18 (Tutankhamun)
TYPE: significance
TAGS: Upper and Lower Egypt, royal legitimacy, magical protection

TEXT:
The vulture and cobra symbolize sovereignty over Upper and Lower Egypt. Worn in life and death, the diadem served as both regalia and magical protection.
