TITLE: Ostrich Hunt Fan – Overview
LANGUAGE: en
MUSEUM: GEM
EXHIBIT: Ostrich Hunt Fan
PERIOD: New Kingdom, Dynasty 18 (Tutankhamun)
TYPE: overview
TAGS: Tutankhamun, ceremonial fan, ostrich hunt, royal power

TEXT:
The Ostrich Hunt Fan of Tutankhamun was found in the Burial Chamber of the king’s tomb. Originally decorated with alternating brown and white ostrich feathers, it was a ceremonial object associated with royal status and display.

TITLE: Ostrich Hunt Fan – Materials and Decoration
LANGUAGE: en
MUSEUM: GEM
EXHIBIT: Ostrich Hunt Fan
PERIOD: New Kingdom, Dynasty 18 (Tutankhamun)
TYPE: details
TAGS: gold, wood, ostrich feathers, royal craftsmanship

TEXT:
The fan was crafted from wood and covered in gold, and once held between thirty and forty-two ostrich feathers. Although the feathers decayed, their arrangement could still be identified during excavation.



ID: gem_tut_ostrich_fan_function_en_003
TITLE: Ostrich Hunt Fan – Royal Meaning
LANGUAGE: en
MUSEUM: GEM
EXHIBIT: Ostrich Hunt Fan
PERIOD: New Kingdom, Dynasty 18 (Tutankhamun)
TYPE: significance
TAGS: royal hunting, control over nature, kingship

TEXT:
In royal ideology, hunting symbolized control over chaos and nature. The fan’s inscriptions state that Tutankhamun himself hunted the ostriches, reinforcing his image as an active and powerful ruler.
