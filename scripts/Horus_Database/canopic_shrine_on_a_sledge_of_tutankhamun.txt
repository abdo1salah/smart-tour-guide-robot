TITLE: Canopic Shrine on a Sledge – Overview
LANGUAGE: en
MUSEUM: GEM
EXHIBIT: Canopic Shrine on a Sledge
PERIOD: New Kingdom, Dynasty 18 (Tutankhamun)
TYPE: overview
TAGS: Tutankhamun, canopic shrine, funerary equipment, nested shrines

TEXT:
This wooden shrine, covered in layers of gold foil, served as the protective housing for Tutankhamun’s canopic chest and the miniature coffins containing his embalmed organs. It rested on a gilded sledge and formed a vital part of the king’s funerary equipment.

ID: gem_tut_canopic_shrine_function_en_003
TITLE: Canopic Shrine on a Sledge – Function and Meaning
LANGUAGE: en
MUSEUM: GEM
EXHIBIT: Canopic Shrine on a Sledge
PERIOD: New Kingdom, Dynasty 18 (Tutankhamun)
TYPE: significance
TAGS: organ protection, afterlife belief, royal burial

TEXT:
In New Kingdom royal burials, nested shrines created a multilayered barrier protecting the king’s body and vital organs. Tutankhamun’s canopic shrine mirrors the design of the four great shrines surrounding his sarcophagus, reinforcing the idea that every aspect of the king’s being required divine protection.
