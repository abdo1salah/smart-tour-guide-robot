TITLE: Outer Coffin of Tutankhamun – Overview
LANGUAGE: en
MUSEUM: GEM
EXHIBIT: Outer Coffin
PERIOD: New Kingdom, Dynasty 18 (Tutankhamun)
TYPE: overview
TAGS: Tutankhamun, coffin, Osiris, royal burial

TEXT:
The Outer Coffin is the largest of three coffins placed inside Tutankhamun’s sarcophagus. It depicts the king as a mummified figure with crossed arms, holding the crook and flail.



ID: gem_tut_outer_coffin_details_en_002
TITLE: Outer Coffin of Tutankhamun – Materials and Design
LANGUAGE: en
MUSEUM: GEM
EXHIBIT: Outer Coffin
PERIOD: New Kingdom, Dynasty 18 (Tutankhamun)
TYPE: details
TAGS: gilded wood, gold foil, anthropoid coffin

TEXT:
The coffin is made of wood covered with plaster, linen, and thin gold foil, while the face, hands, and neck are covered with thicker hammered gold. Its anthropoid form presents the king as the god Osiris.
