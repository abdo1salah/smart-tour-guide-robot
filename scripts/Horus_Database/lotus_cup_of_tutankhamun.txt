TITLE: Lotus Cup of Tutankhamun – Overview
LANGUAGE: en
MUSEUM: GEM
EXHIBIT: Lotus Cup
PERIOD: New Kingdom, Dynasty 18 (Tutankhamun)
TYPE: overview
TAGS: Tutankhamun, lotus cup, rebirth, alabaster

TEXT:
The Lotus Cup of Tutankhamun, often called the Wishing Cup, is a finely carved alabaster vessel shaped as an open white lotus flower. It was found in the tomb of the young pharaoh and is deeply connected to rebirth symbolism.



ID: gem_tut_lotus_cup_details_en_002
TITLE: Lotus Cup – Design and Figures
LANGUAGE: en
MUSEUM: GEM
EXHIBIT: Lotus Cup
PERIOD: New Kingdom, Dynasty 18 (Tutankhamun)
TYPE: details
TAGS: Heh, eternity, ankh, shen sign

TEXT:
The cup features handles shaped as blue lotus flowers supporting baskets with kneeling figures of Heh, god of eternity. Heh holds ankh signs and palm branches resting on tadpoles and shen symbols, representing infinite life and time.



ID: gem_tut_lotus_cup_function_en_003
TITLE: Lotus Cup – Symbolic Meaning
LANGUAGE: en
MUSEUM: GEM
EXHIBIT: Lotus Cup
PERIOD: New Kingdom, Dynasty 18 (Tutankhamun)
TYPE: significance
TAGS: rebirth, sun cycle, afterlife wishes

TEXT:
The lotus symbolized regeneration, as it closed at night and opened at dawn. Inscriptions on the cup express wishes for eternal life, health, and happiness, reinforcing the belief in the king’s rebirth in the afterlife.
