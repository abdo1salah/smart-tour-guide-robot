TITLE: Guardian Statue of Tutankhamun – Khat Headdress (Overview)
LANGUAGE: en
MUSEUM: GEM
EXHIBIT: Guardian Statue with Khat Headdress
PERIOD: New Kingdom, Dynasty 18 (Tutankhamun)
TYPE: overview
TAGS: Tutankhamun, guardian statue, khat headdress, tomb protection

TEXT:
This life-size guardian statue of Tutankhamun stood at the sealed entrance to his Burial Chamber, one of a pair positioned face to face. The two statues may have represented the king and his ka, emphasizing protection and eternal presence at the threshold of the burial space.

PERIOD: New Kingdom, Dynasty 18 (Tutankhamun)
TYPE: details
TAGS: wood core, black resin, gilding, mace, staff, uraeus

TEXT:
The statue has a wooden core coated with black resin and gilded accents. Tutankhamun wears a khat headdress and a pleated royal kilt, and holds a mace and a long staff. A cobra uraeus on the forehead symbolizes royal protection.



ID: gem_tut_guardian_khat_function_en_003
TITLE: Guardian Statue of Tutankhamun – Protective Meaning
LANGUAGE: en
MUSEUM: GEM
EXHIBIT: Guardian Statue with Khat Headdress
PERIOD: New Kingdom, Dynasty 18 (Tutankhamun)
TYPE: significance
TAGS: Osiris, afterlife, guardian figures, tomb doorway

TEXT:
The black coloration links the king to Osiris, god of the afterlife, reinforcing the theme of resurrection. As a guardian figure, the statue protected the tomb entrance and signaled that the king remained divinely empowered even in death.

LANGUAGE: en
MUSEUM: GEM
EXHIBIT: Guardian Statue with Khat Headdress
PERIOD: New Kingdom, Dynasty 18
TYPE: faq
TAGS: visitor questions, ka, tomb guardians

TEXT:
Q: Why were two guardian statues placed at the doorway?
A: To protect the burial chamber and represent the king’s continuing presence.

Q: What is the ka?
A: A spiritual aspect of the person, often translated as life-force.

Q: Why is the statue black?
A: Black is linked to Osiris and rebirth.
