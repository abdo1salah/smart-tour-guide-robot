PERIOD: New Kingdom, Dynasty 18 (Tutankhamun)
TYPE: overview
TAGS: Tutankhamun, perfume jar, lion symbolism, alabaster

TEXT:
This lion-shaped perfume jar was found in the Annex of Tutankhamun’s tomb. Carved from a single piece of Egyptian alabaster, it depicts a crowned lion standing on a tall stool and originally contained perfumed ointment.

TYPE: details
TAGS: alabaster, ivory, gold fittings, New Kingdom art

TEXT:
The lion’s teeth and tongue were made of ivory, while its missing earrings and claws were likely gold. Painted floral and geometric motifs in blue, white, and yellow decorate the vessel, reflecting the high artistic standards of royal workshops.

TITLE: Lion-Shaped Perfume Jar – Symbolic Meaning
LANGUAGE: en
MUSEUM: GEM
EXHIBIT: Lion-Shaped Perfume Jar
PERIOD: New Kingdom, Dynasty 18 (Tutankhamun)
TYPE: significance
TAGS: protection, royal power, funerary symbolism

TEXT:
Lions symbolized strength, protection, and royal authority in ancient Egypt. The aggressive posture of the lion was intended to ward off evil and protect the king, while the perfume inside was associated with purification and renewal.
