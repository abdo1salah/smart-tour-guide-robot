TITLE: Canopic Coffinette of Tutankhamun – Selket
LANGUAGE: en
MUSEUM: GEM
EXHIBIT: Canopic Coffinette (Selket)
PERIOD: New Kingdom, Dynasty 18 (Tutankhamun)
TYPE: overview
TAGS: Tutankhamun, canopic coffinette, Selket, funerary protection

TEXT:
This coffinette is one of four miniature solid-gold coffins created to contain Tutankhamun’s mummified internal organs. It is decorated with delicate inlays of red carnelian and colored glass and presents the king enveloped by the wings of protective goddesses.

ID: gem_tut_canopic_coffinette_selket_function_en_003
TITLE: Canopic Coffinette of Tutankhamun – Function and Guardian
LANGUAGE: en
MUSEUM: GEM
EXHIBIT: Canopic Coffinette (Selket)
PERIOD: New Kingdom, Dynasty 18 (Tutankhamun)
TYPE: significance
TAGS: Selket, intestines, funerary magic, Four Sons of Horus

TEXT:
This coffinette is associated with the goddess Selket, a protector against danger and a healing deity in funerary magic. In Tutankhamun’s burial, Selket was paired with the Son of Horus responsible for guarding the intestines.
