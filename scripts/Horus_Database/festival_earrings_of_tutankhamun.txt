LANGUAGE: en
MUSEUM: GEM
EXHIBIT: Festival Earrings
PERIOD: New Kingdom, Dynasty 18 (Tutankhamun)
TYPE: details
TAGS: heb festival, uraeus, falcon, royal iconography

TEXT:
The heb festival sign symbolizes renewal and the reaffirmation of kingship. The uraeus snakes represent royal protection, while the falcon above links the king to Horus, the divine embodiment of kingship.
