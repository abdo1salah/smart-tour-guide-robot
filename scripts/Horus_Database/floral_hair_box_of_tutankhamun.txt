PERIOD: New Kingdom (Tutankhamun)
TYPE: significance
TAGS: rebirth, fertility, personal magic, funerary belief

TEXT:
Objects containing human hair held strong symbolic meaning in ancient Egypt. Preserved hair could act as a magical substitute for a loved one’s presence and was associated with rebirth and continuity beyond death.
