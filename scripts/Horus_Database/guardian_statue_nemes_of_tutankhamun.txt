TITLE: Guardian Statue of Tutankhamun – Nemes Headcloth (Overview)
LANGUAGE: en
MUSEUM: GEM
EXHIBIT: Guardian Statue with Nemes Headcloth
PERIOD: New Kingdom, Dynasty 18 (Tutankhamun)
TYPE: overview
TAGS: Tutankhamun, guardian statue, nemes headcloth, tomb protection

TEXT:
This life-size guardian statue of Tutankhamun is part of a pair that stood before the sealed doorway to his Burial Chamber. Shown as a powerful royal figure holding a staff and a mace, it functioned as a protective presence at the threshold of the king’s burial space.

LANGUAGE: en
MUSEUM: GEM
EXHIBIT: Guardian Statue with Nemes Headcloth
PERIOD: New Kingdom, Dynasty 18 (Tutankhamun)
TYPE: details
TAGS: nemes, wood core, black resin, gold leaf, mace, staff

TEXT:
Tutankhamun wears the nemes headcloth, the striped royal headdress associated with divine kingship. The statue is carved from wood and coated with black resin and gold leaf, and the king holds a long staff and a mace—symbols of authority and protection.

LANGUAGE: en
MUSEUM: GEM
EXHIBIT: Guardian Statue with Nemes Headcloth
PERIOD: New Kingdom, Dynasty 18 (Tutankhamun)
TYPE: significance
TAGS: Horus, Ra, royal authority, afterlife journey

TEXT:
The nemes headcloth represents royal authority and is strongly linked with gods such as Horus and Ra. Combined with the black resin associated with Osiris and regeneration, the statue presents the king as divinely protected and ready for his journey into the afterlife.
